How a job moves

The Press Run

Five stages, same order every time, from your file to a stack of dry sheets ready to ship.

01 Proof

Digital layout approved, then a single hand-pulled proof for sign-off.

02 Plate

Photopolymer or metal type locked into the chase.

03 Mix

Ink mixed to match, drawn down and checked against your swatch.

04 Run

Sheets fed by hand through the Vandercook, one colour at a time.

05 Dry & Trim

24-hour cure, then trimmed to size and packed flat.
